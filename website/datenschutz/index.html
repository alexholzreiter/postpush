PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy policy.

Information about the processing of personal data when visiting the PostPush website.

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026

1. Controller and website operator

The website is operated by Laura Wagner / klickwerk. The operator's contact details and further provider information are available in the legal notice.

View legal notice

2. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you access the website, GitHub may process technical connection data such as IP addresses, request timestamps, requested URLs, browser information and HTTP status codes. The hosting provider determines the precise log data, retention periods and security procedures.

Depending on the provider's infrastructure, data may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Please consult GitHub's privacy statement for details.

GitHub Privacy Statement

3. Cookies and local storage

The website does not intentionally use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. The language preference is stored in your browser's localStorage under the key postpush-marketing-language, if this key is used by the current website implementation. This allows the selected language to be retained between visits.

Local storage is distinct from HTTP cookies. Applicable legal requirements for storing information on or accessing information from your device must nevertheless be considered.

4. External links and donations

The website links to external services, including GitHub for source code, documentation and issue reporting, and Stripe for optional donations. These services are opened through external links rather than embedded payment or tracking components. When you follow a link, the respective provider processes your request under its own privacy policy.

Stripe Privacy Policy

5. The PostPush software

PostPush is an independent open-source project designed to run on hardware controlled by its users. According to the project's intended architecture, mailbox events, MQTT messages, audio files, speech generation and configuration are processed by the user's own installation rather than by the project website.

The data processed by a self-hosted installation depends on the user's configuration and the services they connect. The privacy practices of that installation are separate from this website's privacy policy.

6. Legal basis and your rights

Where personal data is processed, the applicable legal basis depends on the purpose and circumstances. Processing of technical data may be necessary for the secure and reliable operation of the website, subject to applicable data protection law.

Subject to the conditions of the GDPR, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

Austrian Data Protection Authority
